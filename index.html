Features

  • Auto-moderation

    Detect spam, excessive caps, blacklisted words and unwanted links automatically.

  • Moderation tools

    Kick/ban/mute with case logging, audit entries and configurable punishments.

  • Welcome & Reaction roles

    Custom welcome messages and reaction role setup to automate member onboarding.

  • Slash commands & Prefix

    Full slash command support with easy configuration and permissions.

  • Utility & Fun

    Polls, reminders, info commands, and small games to boost engagement.

Example Commands

/kick <user> [reason]
Kick a user (requires permission).
/ban <user> [reason]
Ban a user.
/mute <user> [duration]
Temporarily mute a member.
/poll <question>
Create a reaction poll quickly.
